# 2.7 — Turn the FAQ into an accordion and expand it

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-06 (see Decisions).
**Branch:** `feat/2.7-faq-accordion` (from `master` after #24 merges; #24 touches `FAQ.razor`)
**Plan reference:** RMT_Website_Plan.docx, task 2.7 (Priority: Medium, Effort: S)

## Goal

A visitor can scan the questions, open just the ones they care about, and find the practical answers (what to wear,
billing, cancellations, payment) without having to email first. The roadmap's "Done when": *questions are
collapsible and keyboard-accessible* and *at least eight common questions are answered*.

## Current state

- `Components/Pages/FAQ.razor`: 3 questions (What is massage therapy? · First massage, what to expect? · How will it
  benefit me?).
  - Each question is an `<h5>` **inside a `<p>`**. That's invalid HTML: the browser closes the `<p>` early, which leaves
    empty paragraphs. The heading levels also jump from h1 to h5.
  - 9 `<br />` inside the answers break sentences mid-line on phones (seen in the 2.8 browser check), and 2 `<hr />` separate the questions.
  - The body sits in `container table-info`. `table-info` is a Bootstrap *table* colour class and does nothing here.
- `PageHeader` ("Frequently asked questions") and `CallToAction` already exist (2.8, 2.2).
- `App.razor:33` already loads `bootstrap.min.js`, and its comment anticipates "the FAQ accordion (2.7)".
- Already confirmed elsewhere and reusable: **prices are plus HST**, and **official receipts are provided for extended
  health insurance claims** (2.5, 2026-10-06).
- Not on the site anywhere yet: address, parking, hours, cancellation policy, payment methods, direct billing.

## Approach

### Collapsible questions: native `<details>`/`<summary>` (recommended)

```html
<details class="faq-item">
  <summary>What should I wear?</summary>
  <div class="faq-answer"><p>…</p></div>
</details>
```

| | `<details>`/`<summary>` | Bootstrap accordion |
|---|---|---|
| Works with no JavaScript (static SSR, JS blocked, before scripts load) | ✅ built into the browser | ❌ all collapsed and unopenable until JS runs |
| Keyboard | ✅ Tab to the question, Enter/Space toggles | ✅ (via JS) |
| Screen reader | ✅ announced as "collapsed/expanded" button, natively | ✅ with correct `aria-expanded` wiring |
| Browser find (Ctrl+F) | ✅ Chrome/Edge open the matching answer | ❌ text in closed panels isn't found |
| Markup | One element per question | `button` + `aria-controls` + `id`s + `data-bs-*` per question |

The roadmap allows either. Native is simpler, more robust, and needs no ids to keep unique. The open/closed marker and
spacing get styled in `FAQ.razor.css`. Several answers can be open at once, which is easier when comparing answers.

### Grouped under h2 headings

Nine questions in one long list is still a lot to scan, so they're grouped (3 × 3):

```
h1  Frequently asked questions                          (PageHeader)
h2  About massage therapy        what it is · benefits · how often to book
h2  Your first visit             what to expect · what to wear · referral needed?
h2  Billing and policies         direct billing · payment methods · cancellation policy
──  CallToAction "Still have questions?" → Contact
```

Parking and accessibility is left out until the address and location details exist (**3.2**).

- This fixes the heading outline (h1 → h2, no jump to h5). Screen-reader users can jump between groups.
- The questions are plain text inside `<summary>`, not headings. A heading inside `<summary>` makes some screen readers
  stop treating it as a button.

### Data: a small list in the page, not markup

```csharp
private record FaqGroup(string Heading, IReadOnlyList<FaqItem> Items);
private record FaqItem(string Question, IReadOnlyList<string> Answer);   // Answer = paragraphs
```

- `@foreach` renders groups and items, so adding a question is one line of data, not a copy-pasted block.
- Answers are lists of **paragraphs**, so there's no `<br>` ever. The text reflows at any width.
- It stays inside `FAQ.razor`'s `@code`. Only this page uses it, so a catalog service (like `IServiceCatalog`) would be
  over-engineering for now. **4.3** (content editable without code) is where it would move to configuration.

### The end of the page

`<CallToAction Heading="Still have questions?" Text="Send a message and Nick will get back to you." ButtonText="Contact Nick" />`
in a full-bleed band, like Services. That matches the roadmap's "Still have questions? Contact us" step.

### Existing answers

The three current answers keep their meaning. They're split into short paragraphs, with the `<br>`s removed and light
copy-editing ("clinically-oriented" → "clinically oriented"). The edited text goes in the PR for your approval.

## Changes during implementation

- **The three existing answers** were split into paragraphs and lightly edited. The vague claim "Massage is a natural
  healing process" was dropped (regulators discourage vague health claims in advertising). The text is shown in the PR for approval.
- **The +/− marker** uses `content: "+" / ""` (empty alt text), so screen readers don't read "plus". Edge's
  accessibility tree confirms each question's name is just the question, with `expanded` false/true.
- **The focus ring** moved from inset (`-2px`) to just outside the row (`2px`). The inset ring touched the first letter.
- **Browser-check notes.** Box-size measurements reported closed answers as "visible", but `isVisible()` confirmed they're
  hidden. Playwright's own role lookup doesn't map `<summary>` to a button, but Edge's real accessibility tree does
  (as a `DisclosureTriangle`). Ctrl+F auto-open can't be driven by automation, so it's left for a manual check.

### Review follow-up (draft PR, before merge)

- **Copy (approved 2026-10-06 after review):**
  - "…assessments to find the location and type of injury or condition" became "…assessments of the area of
    concern to plan your treatment". Communicating a diagnosis is a controlled act RMTs aren't authorized to perform in Ontario.
  - "It can deliver both physical and emotional benefits" was dropped as a vague claim (same reason as "natural healing process").
  - "Evidence-based practice" stays: it's a common professional description and low risk.
  - All nine answers are now pinned word for word.
- **Tests:**
  - The answer-paragraph check could pass on an empty `<p>` (`\S` matched the `<` of `</p>`). It now requires
    text in every paragraph, verified by blanking a paragraph (the test goes red).
  - The collapsed check also confirms every `<details>` was parsed.
  - The CTA is matched by its exact class, and its button text is checked.
  - Multi-paragraph answers are compared paragraph by paragraph. Blazor renders consecutive `<p>` with no whitespace
    between them, so stripping tags glued sentences together.
- The `FAQ.razor` comment now says Ctrl+F auto-open is Chrome/Edge behaviour, not a general fact.
- **Not yet done:** a Safari/VoiceOver check of the `<summary>` styling (only Edge was tested).

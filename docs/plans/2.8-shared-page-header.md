## Goal

Every content page starts the same way: one `<h1>`, an optional subtitle, and a consistent divider and spacing
from CSS. The roadmap's "Done when": *all pages use the same header component* and *no `<hr /><br />` spacing
hacks remain*.

## Approach

### 1. Replace the header block on About, Contact, FAQ, and NotFound

```razor
@* before *@                                        @* after *@
<h1 class="text-center">Contact Us</h1>            <PageHeader Title="Contact us"
<hr />                                                         Subtitle="…" />
<br />
```

Agreed headings:

| Page | `Title` (the h1) | `Subtitle` | `<PageTitle>` (tab) |
|---|---|---|---|
| About | Nicholas Turco, RMT | Registered massage therapist in Guelph, Ontario | About Me (unchanged) |
| Contact | Contact us | Questions or appointment requests? Send a message below. | Contact Us (unchanged) |
| FAQ | Frequently asked questions | (none) | FAQ (unchanged) |
| NotFound | Page not found | (none) | (none yet; added in 3.5) |

- The headings become left-aligned like Services, instead of a mix of centred and left. About loses its oversized
  `display-3` h1, so all pages share one heading scale.
- Only sentence-case changes and "FAQ" → "Frequently asked questions" alter wording. The spelled-out form is clearer
  for screen readers and search, and the nav link and tab stay "FAQ".

### 2. PageHeader owns its `.container`

On Services, the page wraps `PageHeader` in `.container` so its left edge lines up with the content. If every page has
to remember that wrapper, one will forget it. So the component renders its own `.container`, and Services moves
`<PageHeader>` above its content container (avoiding a container nested in a container, which doubles the side padding).

Alternative considered: keep the wrapper in each page. That's rejected because alignment shouldn't depend on every page getting it right.

### 3. Scope of "no spacing hacks" (Q1)

**Recommended: header only.** Remove the `<hr /><br />` under each h1 now. Leave the `<br>`/`<hr>` *inside* the About bio
and FAQ answers to 2.6 and 2.7, which rewrite those bodies anyway. Tidying them now would be throwaway work.
The "no `<hr>`/`<br>`" source test covers Services, Contact, and NotFound now, and About and FAQ are added
to it in 2.6 and 2.7. 2.8 is then *done* for its components, and its second "Done when" is closed by 2.6/2.7.

Alternative: also strip the body `<br>`s in About and FAQ now (text simply reflows). It's small, but 2.6/2.7 replace
those paragraphs entirely.

### 4. CallToAction

Already built and in use. Adding it to About and FAQ is part of 2.6 ("Book with Nick") and 2.7 ("Still have
questions?"). Contact doesn't get one, because it would link to itself.

## Changes during implementation

- **FAQ and Not Found** bodies weren't in any `.container`, so they started further left than the aligned heading.
  Both are now wrapped in `.container`. This is layout only; their content is unchanged for 2.7 and 3.5.
- **Contact** used `container w-75`, a centred 75%-wide box, which can't line up with the heading. `w-75` is removed.
  This also gives the `w-25` inputs a little more room on phones, though they're still too narrow (3.1).
- `ServicesMarkupTests` became `PageMarkupTests` (a theory over Services, ContactUs, and NotFound).
- The browser check also confirmed the contact form: an empty submit shows 3 validation messages, and a valid submit
  clears them and shows the send-failure message (Mailgun pointed at a dead port). Stale validation text stays
  visible for about 4s while "Sending…" is shown. That's existing behaviour, noted for 3.1.
